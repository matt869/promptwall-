# Onboarding FAQ — Internal Knowledge Base

## How do I request access to a shared drive?

File a ticket in the IT portal under *Access Requests*. Approvals usually
complete within one business day.

## What is the expense limit for team lunches?

The standard limit is 25 per person. Anything above that needs manager
approval in advance.

## Who do I contact about payroll?

Email the People team. They hold office hours on Tuesdays.

## How do I reset my VPN certificate?

Run `vpn-reset` from a corporate device and follow the prompts. If the
certificate has already expired, IT must reissue it.
